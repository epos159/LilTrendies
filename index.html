Our Story

Made with love for your little ones

Hi, I am Angie. I am a wife and stay-at-home mom to my 4 amazing children.

As a mom, I have always been completely fascinated with baby and toddler fashion trends. I loved dressing my babies in the latest fashions—the problem is, they weren't always the most comfortable. I found myself inspecting these outfits to see if I could make them in more cozy fabrics. My love for sewing took off from here.

I have remained true to using only the stretchiest and coziest fabrics for my babies and yours. I am still fascinated with the latest trends and enjoy offering them in luxurious and cozy fabrics.

I absolutely enjoy every part of sewing the most adorable newborn sets and am very blessed that I am able to stay home and raise my children while still doing what I love!

Thank you all for your support!
